# Trademark guidelines

"Crustates", the product names (Tide, Reef, Current, Molt, Cast, Barnacle, Spawn) and the crab logo identify the official Crustates project. The code is open source; the names and logo are not a licence to look official.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name and logo to refer to Crustates: in articles, talks, videos, tutorials and docs.
- Use the "Runs on Crustates", "Powered by Crustates" and other badges on projects that actually run on or integrate with Crustates.
- Name community plugins, apps and tools "<name> for Crustates" or "crustates-<name>".
- Show the logo unmodified, in the official colours or in single-colour versions from this repo.

## Please don't

- Ship a fork, a modified build or a hosted service under the name Crustates or with the crab logo. Rename it and say it is based on Crustates.
- Suggest your product, company or event is official, endorsed or certified when it is not.
- Change the logo's shape, colours or proportions, or combine it with your own mark.
- Use the names or logo in domain names, app store names or social accounts in a way that could be confused with the project.

## Questions

Ask at hello@crustates.eu. When in doubt, ask; we usually say yes.
